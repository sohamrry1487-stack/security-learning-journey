# 🔐 Security Learning Journey

### From curious beginner to capable security practitioner.

**Learn it. Understand it. Practise it. Share it.**

Welcome to my cybersecurity learning journey! 👋

I'm a second-year Cyber Security engineering student from India, building my skills through hands-on practice, experimentation, and continuous learning.

This repository is where I document that process — the concepts I learn, the problems I struggle with, the tools I explore, and the projects I build.

But this isn't just a personal notebook.

You don't need to know everything before you begin. You just need to be willing to learn, experiment, ask questions, and keep going.

## 🎯 The Mission

My goal is to build a strong foundation in cybersecurity, improve my independent problem-solving skills, and turn what I learn into something useful for others.

I'm focusing on three things:

- **Understanding over memorisation:** Knowing why something works, not just which command to run.
- **Practice over passive learning:** Applying concepts through experiments, exercises, and projects.
- **Sharing over gatekeeping:** Making difficult concepts easier for other beginners to understand.

## 🗺️ The Road Ahead

**Phase 1 — Foundations**
Linux, networking, Python, and essential computing concepts.

**Phase 2 — Exploration**
CTFs, OSINT, digital forensics, web security, and other cybersecurity domains.

**Phase 3 — Building**
Small projects that turn theoretical knowledge into practical skills.

**Phase 4 — Deeper Practice**
More complex challenges, improved methodologies, and focused learning based on experience.

This roadmap will evolve as I learn more and discover where my interests lead.

## 🧠 My Learning Philosophy

When I encounter a new concept, I aim to:

1. Learn what it is and why it matters.
2. Understand how it works.
3. Experiment in a safe, authorised environment.
4. Document what I discovered and what confused me.
5. Explain it clearly enough that another beginner can learn from it.
